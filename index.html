Live 2026

ChanceClub

A party-games platform for groups. One person opens a lobby, everyone else joins with a six-letter code, and the host picks a game from 17: Wordle, Word Bomb, Imposter, Two Truths and a Lie, Trivia, Draw & Guess, Charades, and six deciders for the arguments.

  • Live lobbies with one scoreboard for the whole night, plus tournament mode with placement points.
  • Three languages: English, Arabic (Egyptian and Standard, full RTL) and German.
  • Guests join by code with no account and play in seconds.
  • Built end to end: front end, API, database, realtime rooms and deploy.
  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Laravel
  • PHP 8.3
  • Postgres
  • WebSockets
